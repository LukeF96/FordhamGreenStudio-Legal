Common questions

  • How do I share my wedding with my partner? From the Home screen, tap "Create Invite Code" and share the code with your partner — they can enter it from the "Join your partner's wedding" link on their own sign-in screen.
  • How do I delete my account? Go to Account & Security in the app and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your data and cannot be undone.
  • Is my data private? Yes — your wedding is only visible to you and the partner you invite. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
